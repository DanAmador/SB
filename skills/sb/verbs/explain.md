# /sb explain [thing]

The owner lost the thread. Retell it in plain words and stop. Read-only: nothing is written,
claimed or resolved.

1. **Pick the thing.** The one the owner named; else the last question you asked them; else the
   current map (`sbmap maps --repo <repo>`).
2. **Read just enough.** A map: `sbmap info <map>`. A ticket: `sbmap show <map> <NNN>`. A question:
   your own last message, plus the ticket it came from.
3. **Retell it** in short sentences, as if to a friend who was not there, by
   [Plain words](../SKILL.md#plain-words): a ticket is its name as a link, and you say what the
   owner will *see* or *feel* when it is done. Words the owner already used stay as they said them.
4. **Shape.** Line 1 is the point. At most 5 lines, then one ➡️ next step (a thing they can do, or a
   question they can answer with "yes"). Then stop: no offer to explain more, no recap.
